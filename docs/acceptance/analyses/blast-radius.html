The first table counts OUT-connection Files only (the endpoints we dial); its Subscriptions/Applications/Domains/Partners columns are distinct counts over those Files, applications and partners by the site-wide UNION attribution. The redundancy and sharing views count each partner's distinct recorded endpoints over ALL its Files — for an inbound flow that is the partner's recorded source address, and a partner with no endpoint recorded anywhere is the inbound-only class. "Sole endpoint for" flags the hosts that are the ONLY recorded endpoint of at least one partner: losing that address strands those partners entirely.
