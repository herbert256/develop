An account is a partner relation, and one relation often carries several flows. The shapes are few: a Mailbox pair (exactly UC2 + UC4) is the partner's inbound mailbox — ONE connection used to drop and pick up, which is why those pages carry the shared-connection notes; an Outbound pair (exactly UC1 + UC3) is the outbound mirror — we push to and poll the same partner; an Outbound fan-out runs several UC1/UC3 flows over one relation. Both directions on one account does not occur for real partners — the synthetic ST monitor account is the exception by design. Inbound sharing beyond the pair (two UC2s, two UC4s) does not occur at all.
