Retry and Resubmit split Recovered by HOW the File got through: Retry = the platform's automatic retry delivered it, Resubmit = an operator resubmitted it (one of its legs carries the Resubmitted flag) — the Top view's Automatic and Manual. Recovered % = the share of that subscription's Files (in the whole loaded window) that needed a retry to get through — a high share on a busy flow points at a flaky endpoint that succeeds on the second try. The Files column counts ALL of the subscription's Files, whatever their outcome; only subscriptions with at least one recovered File are listed.
