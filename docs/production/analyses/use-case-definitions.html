What each UC<n> prefix means. UC1–UC4 bridge one partner to our internal application; UC5–UC8 are direct relays with no CFT leg, one per source/target push-pull combination — each links two partners, which is why a relay account is named domain_partner_partner rather than the domain_application_partner of UC1–UC4; UC6 and UC7 have a published template but no subscriptions yet (their rows are template-derived). Trigger is what starts the flow: OpsWise = our OpsWise automation initiates (UC1, UC8), Partner = the partner connects in (UC2, UC4, UC7), Cronjob = a Quartz receive scheduler polls the partner (UC3, and the pull side of UC5/UC6). The operational counts per use case are on the Use Case traffic tab; the flow templates behind them are in the Templates table below.
