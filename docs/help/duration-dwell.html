Store-and-forward

What it shows

  • The wait inside SecureTransport between a File’s inbound leg ending and its outbound leg starting (the dwell): its distribution, per subscription and per day.
  • The summary line gives the measurable Files and their median, p95 and max dwell. (The whole-trip Duration distribution is on the Duration page.)

Columns

  • Dwell-time distribution — Dwell / Files / Share, bands from < 1 s to over 5 min.
  • Dwell by subscription — Subscription / Files / Avg dwell / Max dwell, slowest first; a row drills to its 10 newest Files.
  • Gap per day — Date / Files / Min / Median / Max / P25 / P75 / P95 / P99 of the in→out gap, under the inbound date.

Rules

  • Dwell by subscription: rows tint by the subscription’s result colour.
  • Dwell = the earliest outbound start minus the latest inbound completion (start + duration). A File forwarded before its inbound leg finished (overlapping legs) is excluded.
  • Gap per day counts only delivered (Processed) Files with exactly one Inbound and one Outbound record, both Processed; its figures are not additive, so a narrowed range blanks the total row.
  • Percentiles use the nearest-rank method; Median is P50.

Tips

  • A growing dwell, invisible in both leg durations, is an early warning of queue pressure or a backed-up outbound target.
