Monitor

What it shows

  • The end-to-end monitor: a file dropped on the CFT every 15 minutes travels through all four use cases, with ourselves as the partner, back to the CFT.
  • Three views cut each trip into disjoint segments, so an incident shows in the segment owning it; each draws P50 / P90 / P98 bands per slot.

Tabs

  • Monitor CFT pickup — from the drop on the CFT (the time in its name) to the start of the inbound UC1 leg into ST: CFT pickup plus PeSIT delivery. Healthy: about a minute.
  • Monitor duration — from entering ST (UC1 inbound leg) to the hand-off back to CFT (UC3 outbound leg): the whole loop. The UC3 poll fires at :05, so it is floored at about 5 minutes.
  • Monitor staging — from entering ST to staged for pickup (the UC2 inbound routing leg): the loop without the poll wait, so ST-internal drift shows here first. Healthy: 1–2 minutes.

Rules

  • Source: the monitor_ccyymmdd_hhmmss.txt files of the INFRA_ST-MONITOR_INFRA subscriptions, joined on the name; the earliest leg per side, so a retry never shifts a measurement.
  • A file that never completed counts as 1 hour (or the slowest completed file, when longer); one younger than an hour against the newest monitor row is skipped.
  • Each view fits its own duration scale; intervals 4 hours … 1 day; Line / Bar / Solid.
  • The page and its top-bar link exist only when the transfer log carries monitor traffic.

Tips

  • A slot click opens that day’s day page.
