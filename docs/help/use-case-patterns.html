Use Case patterns

What it shows

Every account grouped by its Use Case pattern: the mix of its configured subscriptions, counted per UC prefix. Accounts with the same mix share one row, answering “which subscription set-ups exist, and how common is each?”

Columns

  • Pattern — the UC tokens in numeric order, each with its subscription count in parentheses: an account with UC2_SI-VPS-VDN_SI-VPS-VDN and UC4_SI-VPS-VDN_SI-VPS-VDN reads UC2 (1) UC4 (1).
  • Accounts — loads collapsed to a count line (“36 accounts”, the coverage pages’ member-cell mechanism); click to expand the alphabetical list, one account per line linking its detail page; click again to collapse. Sorting orders by the count.

Rules

  • Account → subscription pairs come from the FlowManager configuration export: the data/flow-manager/xref/ pair cache built from subscriptions.json — configured subscriptions, whether or not they ever appear in the logs.
  • Each subscription contributes the leading UC<n> token of its name; without a UC prefix it counts under (none).
  • An account with no subscriptions has no pattern and is not listed.
  • Rows are ordered by how many accounts share the pattern, most first. Single-subscription patterns (UC1 (1), UC4 (1)) dominate; the long tail is the poll-heavy accounts with many UC3 subscriptions.
  • The total row counts the distinct patterns and the accounts listed.
