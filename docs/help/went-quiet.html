Went quiet

What it shows

  • The silence report: subscriptions that carried Files and then stopped, and accounts idle long against their own cadence. Nothing fails when a flow stops, so no other report shows it.

Tabs

  • Subscriptions — boxes Subscriptions with Files and Went quiet; per quiet subscription (links its detail page) Days ago last traffic, longest-quiet first.
  • Accounts — First / Last activity, Active days, Median gap (typical spacing between active days), Days idle, Idle ÷ gap (missed cycles), Files. The Total row counts the stale and overdue accounts.

Rules

  • A subscription is quiet when its newest File is more than 7 days before the last day in the data. Every outcome counts as traffic: the question is “is anything happening”, not “is it working”.
  • A subscription that never carried a File cannot go quiet; the Entities Not seen view lists those.
  • Accounts: Days idle 7+ is tinted (stale), and so is Idle ÷ gap 2 or more (overdue); an account seen on one day has no gap (“-”). Files without an account are left out.
  • Both measure from the window end, never today; always the full period.

Tips

  • A daily account idle 3 days (ratio 3.0) is more alarming than a weekly one idle 5 days (0.7).
  • Health is judged on Failed Subscriptions.
