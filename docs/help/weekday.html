Load by Weekday

What this shows

One row per day of week (Monday through Sunday): how many calendar days of that weekday were observed, the Files count, its average per day, the Error/OK split, the failure rate, and the volume. This is the view for spotting weekday-vs-weekend batch patterns — for example, a partner that only delivers Monday-Friday will show near-zero Saturday/Sunday rows.

How it's computed

Each record is assigned to a weekday from its date (via a Julian-day-number calculation, so it's portable and doesn't depend on the date command). "Days" counts how many distinct calendar dates of that weekday fall within the date range — e.g. if the data spans 10 weeks, "Monday" will normally show 10 — and "Avg/day" divides the weekday's count by that figure, not by a fixed 7.
