Blast radius

What this shows

What stops when a remote host dies: one row per outbound endpoint we dial, counting the Files, volume, subscriptions, applications, domains and partner organisations routed over it. A red row is the sole recorded endpoint of at least one partner — no second address exists to fail over to.

The redundancy table classes every seen partner by its distinct recorded endpoints across all its Files: single, multiple, or none recorded (inbound-only — the partner dials us, a legitimate class rather than a gap). The last table lists endpoints shared by several partner organisations, where one address outage crosses relationship boundaries.
