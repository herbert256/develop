AV Scan

What it shows

The anti-virus (ICAP) verdict of every File, from its first Inbound leg (the scan runs on entry): Allowed, Blocked, Not performed or Other. The intro gives the allowed / blocked / not-performed totals.

Tabs

  • Breakdown — the overall split; Allowed tinted green, Blocked red.
  • Per day — outcomes per day.
  • Per protocol — the first Inbound leg’s protocol, how the file entered: pesit = from CFT, ssh/ftp = a partner delivering in.
  • Blocked — every blocked file, newest first, capped at 200 (a note counts the hidden ones; an explicit empty-state row when none).
  • Not performed — why arrivals skipped the scan; click a row for its 10 most recent Files.
  • Not first inbound — the check on the scan-on-entry model: real verdicts (Allowed/Blocked/Error) on any other leg.

Rules

  • One chronological walk over each File’s rows; the first Inbound leg’s verdict counts; no Inbound leg = no scan, absent.
  • Outcomes are parsed from the free-text “ICAP Details” column; Other = Error/Unknown and unrecognised text.
  • Not performed reasons:
    • Before scanning was active — ahead of the window’s first Allowed verdict (boundary derived from the data each run).
    • Failed or empty — the receive aborted or brought 0 bytes.
    • Encrypted — .pgp/.enc, the scanner cannot see inside.
    • Oversized — above the ICAP scan-size cap.
    • Other — no known reason; should sit near 0, growth = a new skip reason to investigate.
  • Not first inbound: legitimate are inbound retries (a re-entry is re-scanned) and the UC2 staging re-entry (Inbound routing); an Outbound row would break the model; the outbound “Scanning was not performed” boilerplate is excluded.
