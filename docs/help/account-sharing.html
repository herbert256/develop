Account sharing

What it shows

Accounts serving more than one subscription, one row each, and the shape of the sharing (an account is a partner relation that often carries several flows, in a few recurring shapes).

Kinds

  • Mailbox pair — exactly UC2 + UC4: the partner’s inbound mailbox, one connection to drop (UC4) and pick up (UC2). Hence the UC2/UC4 pages’ shared-connection notes; such pairs are Twins.
  • Outbound pair — exactly UC1 + UC3: we push to and poll the same partner.
  • Outbound fan-out — several UC1/UC3 flows over one relation, up to dozens.
  • Inbound (irregular) — inbound sharing beyond the pair (two UC2s, two UC4s): the hybrid production accounts.
  • Both directions — inbound and outbound on one account. Real partners never; the synthetic ST end-to-end monitor account is built so on purpose.

Columns

  • Account (links its detail page), Kind, Per use case (subscriptions per UC), Subscriptions (the full list; three or more fold behind an ellipsis, click to expand).

Rules

  • Configuration only: the account → subscription pairs of the FlowManager export (xref cache); no log data, identical however much traffic ran.
  • Kind reads the names’ UC<n> prefix (UC1/UC3 = we connect out, UC2/UC4 = the partner connects in); a flow without one (hybrid production) uses the use case derived from its configuration.
  • Only shared accounts are listed; the single-subscription majority is counted in the boxes above.
  • Ordered by kind, then size, largest first.
  • A login usually rides its account 1-to-1 (“same account” = “same FE login”); a hybrid production account shares one login across its flows or carries several FE logins, one per flow (the multi-FE shape).
