Connection efficiency

What this shows

A session is one technical connection — an SFTP login or PeSIT session — identified by the log's Session ID column; one session can carry many Files, but on this platform 95% carry exactly one.

The league lists User-initiated sessions only (the partner controls the reconnect behaviour) for accounts with 200+ sessions, least efficient first: a ratio of 1.00 at tens of thousands of sessions means a fresh connection per file, and batching would cut real overhead. Connection storms shows the most sessions one account started inside a single minute.

The failure anatomy splits bad sessions into all-Error (a connection that achieved nothing), mixed OK+Error, and retry marathons — 8+ records hammering one File, all Error, inside a single connection. A session is attributed to its first record's account.
