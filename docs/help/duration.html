Transfer Duration

What it shows

How long Files take, per day: two tables side by side — the percentiles and the min / avg / median / max. The intro states the overall min, median, p95, p99 and max over the window.

Columns

  • Percentiles (left) — p10, p25, p50 (median), p75, p90, p95, p98, p99 and p100 per day. A percentile is the duration this share of the day’s Files finished at or below: p95 = 95% were at least this fast.
  • p100 — the day’s longest File, the same value as Max; next to p99, the gap says how far the worst case sits from the rest.
  • Min / Avg / Median / Max (right) — the shortest, mean, middle and longest duration per calendar day.

Rules

  • OK transfers (default, the button row) counts delivered (Processed) Files only: Error transfers — mostly instant 0-byte attempts — would flatten every statistic toward zero.
  • All transfers counts every File with a measured duration; a failed transfer’s duration is how long it ran before failing (a 2-hour timeout, say), so that view’s Max can far exceed the OK view’s.
  • A File = all records sharing one CoreId; its duration is the wall-clock span from first record start to last record end, in milliseconds — store-and-forward gap and retry idle included, not the sum of the record durations.
  • Raw log durations are compound and mixed-unit (574 ms, 1.314 s, 1 min 0.550 s); they are normalised to milliseconds when the caches are built and shown here in whole seconds, minutes or hours.
  • Percentiles use the nearest-rank method and resist the single outlier that dominates Max.
  • Per-day min / avg / max and percentiles are not additive: a narrowed date range keeps each day row but blanks those columns in the total row (“–”); the Files count re-totals normally.

Tips

  • Own pages in this group: Longest Files (the 50 slowest transfers), Duration distribution & Store-and-forward (histograms; the buckets re-count over the date range) and Slowest subscriptions (the 25 flows with the highest p95; Median and p95 stay full-period, Files and Max re-aggregate).
