Waiting

What it shows

  • UC2 Files staged for the partner to collect: which are still waiting, how long, and how fast partners usually collect.
  • A Waiting File’s last leg is the Inbound routing leg into the pickup area, with no collect leg after it and its staged copy not yet deleted — not an error.

Columns

  • Waiting now (first table) — per subscription: Waiting Files (opens its list of staged Files), Oldest staged, Waiting for, Newest staged, Collected.
  • Files waiting the longest — the 50 oldest staged Files. Rows orange, the File colour of a Waiting File.
  • Pickup wait — collected files — median, average, max; within 1 h, 1–24 h, over 24 h.
  • Staged backlog over time — the uncollected inventory per day; its last day equals the Waiting count.
  • Will expire next — Waiting Files staged 9+ days: call the partner today.
  • Pickup wait percentiles per partner — median, p95, max, Waited over 8 days (near-misses).
  • Pickup wait per week — median and average per staged week for the 10 busiest subscriptions.

Rules

  • Waiting for runs from staging to the data’s last record. The nightly retention sweep deletes a staged copy after ~11 days: the File becomes Expired.
  • Pickup wait = staging end to the first collect; it is left out of the UC2 Duration figures. Collected = the delivered (Processed) Files with a pickup wait, in every table.
  • A partner counts every File of its subscriptions, so partner totals can exceed the collected total.
  • Always the full period: Waiting is the state at the window end.

Tips

  • On a subscription’s list, the first 5 CoreIds open the File page.
