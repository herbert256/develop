What it shows

  • Reports — the Start page, then one card per group of the Reports menu, named as the menu names it, with its report count and its reports in menu order.
  • Dashboards — the Dashboard, and the Monitor when this site has one.
  • Data pages & tools — Per-day pages (how many day pages exist), Entity detail pages (the count per type — Accounts, Subscriptions, Logins, Hosts, Logical, Partners, Applications, Domains, BL — each linking its Entities page) and Tools.
