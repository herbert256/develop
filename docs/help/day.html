Activity per Day

What this shows

One row per calendar day: the Files count, its Error/OK split, the day's volume, and the first and last time seen that day. This is the most basic activity-over-time view on the site — Per Week, Load by Hour and Load by Weekday all re-bucket the same underlying activity by a different calendar dimension.

How it's computed

Every day in the full calendar span between the first and last record is shown, even one with zero activity: a day with no Files gets an explicit "0" row with - in the First/Last Time columns, so a quiet day is visually distinct from a missing one rather than simply absent from the table.

A day at either edge of the log collection window is flagged (partial start) or (partial end) when its first time is after 02:00 or its last time is before 22:00 — a sign that the export didn't cover the whole day, so its numbers should not be compared directly with a full day.
