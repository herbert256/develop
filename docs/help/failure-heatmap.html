Failure heatmap

What it shows

  • When Files fail: by hour of day, by weekday, and an hour × weekday grid. A summary line under the tables gives the Files, the Files in error and their share.

Columns

  • By hour of day (the first table) — Hour, Files, Error, Error %, and a Failures bar relative to the worst hour.
  • By weekday — the same per Weekday, Monday first.
  • Hour × weekday failure heatmap — 24 rows × 7 weekdays, each cell the Files in error for that hour on that weekday, tinted by quartile (darker = more).

Rules

  • A File is placed by its start hour and weekday; it is in error when its outcome is Failed or Expired.
  • Hours and weekdays without errors are not listed.
  • Every table follows the date filter; the heatmap re-sums and re-tints its cells.

Tips

  • Click an Error count for that bucket’s 10 most recent failed Files.
  • One dominant window makes every other cell pale: narrow the range to compare a normal week with an incident. Failures in one hour, or every Monday morning, point at a schedule or upstream feed.
  • Volume over the same axes: Activity.
