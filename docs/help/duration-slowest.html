Slowest subscriptions

What it shows

The 25 subscriptions whose Files take longest, ranked by the p95 of their wall-clock duration — first record start to last record end, store-and-forward wait and retry idle included. p95 rather than the maximum, so one freak transfer does not crown a flow.

Columns

  • Subscription — linked to its detail page.
  • Files — the Files counted.
  • Median, p95 (the ranking value), Max.
  • The Total row says how many subscriptions the scope has in all.

Rules

  • OK transfers (default) ranks delivered Files only; All transfers adds the failed ones, whose duration is how long they ran before giving up — a flow with long-hanging timeouts climbs the list there.
  • Duration = the File’s wall-clock span in milliseconds, gaps included, not the sum of its record durations.
  • From the logical-transfer cache (one row per CoreId): per subscription the sorted durations of its Files in the scope, p50 and p95 read at the nearest rank, then the 25 highest p95. Ties break on the Files count, then the name.
  • A From/To range re-aggregates Files and Max from the per-day buckets each row carries; Median and p95 keep their full-period value (percentiles cannot be re-derived from daily figures).

Tips

  • Per-day statistics and percentiles: Transfer Duration; the individual slowest transfers: Longest Files; the histogram of all durations: Duration distribution & Store-and-forward.
