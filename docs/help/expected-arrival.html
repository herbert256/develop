Punctuality › Rhythm

What it shows

  • Each subscription judged against its own rhythm: a cadence model from the gaps between its active days (days with at least one File), and an OVERDUE verdict when the current silence breaks it.

Columns

  • Overdue by their own rhythm (the first table, red rows) and On rhythm (most-silent first, the closest to overdue on top): Files, Active days, Median gap, p90 gap, Last seen, Days silent, Verdict (with the flow’s limit in days).
  • Weekday-locked flows — 75%+ of the active days on one Weekday: Share, Active days, Last seen, Next expected (the first such weekday after the last arrival; (missed) = already passed without a File).

Rules

  • A flow needs 5+ active days to be modelled.
  • OVERDUE when the days silent exceed max(p90 gap + 1, median gap + 2), measured against the newest day in the data.
  • Gaps run between active days, so a flow sending 40 Files every Monday still has a 7-day rhythm.
  • Verdict long-period marks a median gap of 20+ days: one skipped period takes weeks to detect.
  • Always the full period.

Tips

  • Unlike Went quiet (one 7-day cutoff for all): a weekly flow is not late after 5 days, a twice-daily one is overdue long before day 7.
