Punctuality › Rhythm

What it shows

Each subscription judged against its own arrival rhythm: a cadence model built from the gaps between its active days (days with at least one File) — median and p90 gap, current silence, and an OVERDUE verdict when the silence breaks the pattern.

Sections

  • Overdue — flows silent longer than their own limit, measured against the newest day in the data.
  • Within limit — every other modelable flow, most-silent first: the top rows are the closest to going overdue. long-period marks a median gap of 20 days or more (monthly-style cadence): one skipped period takes weeks to detect, by design.
  • Weekday-locked — flows with at least 75% of their active days on ONE weekday (a weekly batch with a fixed slot). Next expected = the first such weekday after the last arrival; (missed) = that day already lies inside the observed window and nothing arrived.

Rules

  • Modelable = 5 or more active days; flows with fewer are left out (the headline says how many).
  • Gaps are between consecutive ACTIVE days, so a flow sending 40 files every Monday still has a 7-day gap pattern.
  • OVERDUE when the current silence exceeds max(p90 gap + 1, median gap + 2) days — a margin above the flow’s own historical worst-normal interval.
  • Unlike Went quiet (one fixed 7-day cutoff for every flow) and the Arrival time tab (a time-of-day model for daily flows): here a weekly flow is not quiet after 5 days, and a twice-daily flow is overdue long before day 7.
