Recovered flows

What this shows

The good-news mirror of "From green to red": every subscription whose latest File is OK but that went through a red episode earlier in the window — at least one day ending on a failed File. The episode shown is the most recent run of consecutive active days that each ended red; "Recovered on" is the first active day after it.

Outage days span the episode's calendar days; the Error count drills into the episode's 10 most recent failures, the OK count into the freshest successes since — the fastest way to confirm a fix actually took. Waiting counts as OK and Expired as Error, the same rule that colors the subscription site-wide. The table always shows the full period: narrowing the range would fabricate or hide recoveries.
