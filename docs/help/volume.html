Transfer Volume

What it shows

How much data moves and which way, on four tabs; record counts sit next to the byte totals.

Tabs

  • Per day — volume and Files per calendar day.
  • By direction — Inbound vs Outbound per row.
  • Top accounts — the busiest accounts by volume, with each account’s share of the total.
  • Trend — per-subscription growers, shrinkers and flows gone silent.

Rules

  • Per day and Top accounts count Files (logical transfers from _files.tsv), each file’s size counted once per File, not once per row: the size of the file that moved, not the sum of its Inbound and Outbound legs.
  • By direction is the exception: physical rows from the parse cache, split Inbound / Outbound, with their own Error/OK and volume figures; it shows the direction split the File view cannot.
  • A transfer writes its size on both rows, so the By direction total is roughly twice the logical total (a note under the table says so). Click a count for that outcome’s 10 most recent transfers.
  • Trend splits the window in half and compares each flow’s Files/volume across the halves.
  • Gone silent = 10+ Files in the first half, then nothing in the window’s final week. Days silent counts to the dataset’s last day, not today. The same signal per account, against its own cadence, is on Went quiet.
  • Growers = 4x+ more Files in the second half (20+ Files there); new = no first-half activity at all, a flow that started mid-window (First seen dates every flow’s absolute first appearance).
