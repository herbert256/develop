Transfer Volume

What this shows

Data volume (bytes moved) across three tabs: Per day (volume and Files per calendar day), By direction (a per-row split between Inbound and Outbound), and Top accounts (the busiest accounts by volume, with each account's share of the total). Record counts are shown alongside the byte totals for context.

How it's computed

The Per day and Top accounts tabs count Files — logical transfers from _files.tsv — with each file's size counted once per File, not once per row. This is what "volume" should mean: the size of the file that moved, not the sum of its Inbound and Outbound legs.

The By direction tab is the one exception on this page: it counts physical rows from the parse cache, split Inbound vs Outbound, with its own Error/OK and volume figures. Because a single transfer writes its file size on both its Inbound and its Outbound row, this table's total volume is roughly twice the logical total shown on the other two tabs — a note under the table restates this. It is included so you can see the split by direction, which the logical File view (one outcome per transfer) cannot show.

About this report

How much data moves and which way it is heading: volume per day, the direction split, the top accounts — and the trend view: the window split in half and each flow's Files/volume compared across the halves, so growers, shrinkers and flows gone silent stand out.
