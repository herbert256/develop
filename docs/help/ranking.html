Ranking

What it shows

Where every entity stands against the others of its own type on the five metrics its detail page ranks it by.

Each metric is two columns, the position and the value behind it, a divider between the pairs. Sorted on the Files position by default; every column sorts.

Columns

  • Files — most Files first; #1 is the busiest of its type.
  • Volume — most bytes first (a flow can be #1 by volume and unremarkable by count).
  • Errors — highest error percentage first: #1 is the worst; a flawless entity sorts last.
  • Duration — shortest average transfer first (#1 = fastest).
  • Throughput — highest bytes per second first (#1 = fastest).

Rules

  • Ranks never cross types; the second button row picks one of the nine entity types.
  • Only entities seen with real transfers are ranked; a server-log-only (blue) entity has no metric and is left out, as on its own page. Never timed: - for Duration and Throughput, still ranked on the other three.
  • Positions are not computed here: each detail page writes its five positions to a sidecar as it renders; this page collates them — the same numbers by construction.
  • A tie shares a position (the next ones are skipped).
  • From/To re-ranks: narrowing recomputes every value and position over the selected days alone; an entity with nothing in range drops out of the table and the population. Duration and Throughput average the timed transfers in range. Widening back restores the baked figures exactly.

Tips

  • Every row of an entity’s Ranking box links here: the page opens at that type, sorted on the clicked metric (#1 on top), turned to the page holding the entity and scrolled to its outlined row — the neighbourhood shows whether the field is packed or the entity an outlier.
  • Such a link always opens at the full period (the clicked position is a full-period figure); your remembered range is left alone, and picking a new one here saves as usual.
