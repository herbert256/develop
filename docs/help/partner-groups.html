Partner group

What it shows

  • One page per partner group: several partner codes (the last part of logical flow names) that the configuration ties to one organisation, named by its codes joined with underscores (GBA_TT_XXLLNC).
  • Opened from the 🔗 icon after a group’s name on Entities › Partners and on the Partners coverage page of the home status table.
  • Three boxes: Member partners, Connection (the group’s configured side: in, out or both) and Merge facts.

Columns

  • Member partner codes — Partner code · Accounts behind the code (the accounts of the logical flows it comes from, up to eight).
  • Why these partners are one group — Partners (the pair of codes) · How they are linked · Evidence (one representative fact, “+N more” for the rest).

Rules

  • Codes merge when their logical flows connect to the same configured host, whitelist the same IP, or one partner’s host resolves to an IP the other whitelists. Merges are transitive.
  • Every merge is derived from the configuration. A known variant spelling is rewritten to its canonical code in input/logical_partners.txt before merging, so it never forms a group.
