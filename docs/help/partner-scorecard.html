Partner scorecard

What this shows

One number per partner relation with at least 100 Files, worst first. The score starts at 100 minus the partner's Error % (the heaviest weight) and deducts for a worsening 14-day error trend, a UC2 average pickup wait over 24 hours, transfer legs on weak security parameters (ssh-rsa or TLSv1.2), a single outbound endpoint, and 14+ days of silence — the NOTE on the page states the exact weights. Every component is its own column, so a low score is checkable per row.

The concentration boxes (top-1/3/10 share, Gini) cover all seen partners and show how heavily the estate leans on a few organisations. The side-by-side top-10 tables expose the volume-vs-count blind spot: the biggest byte mover is not the biggest file counter.
