Partner scorecard

What it shows

One composite 0–100 health score per partner relation with at least 100 Files, worst first, every ingredient in its own column so a low score is checkable per row.

Concentration boxes show how heavily the estate leans on a few organisations.

Rules

  • The score starts at 100 minus the Error % (Failed or Expired Files — the heaviest weight by far).
  • Minus 0.5 points per percentage point the last-14-days Error % worsened against the 14 days before (capped at 15; improving never adds).
  • Minus 5 points when the average UC2 partner pickup wait exceeds 24 h.
  • Minus up to 5 points, scaled by the share of transfer legs on a weak security parameter (ssh-rsa host key or TLSv1.2).
  • Minus 3 points when everything we send the partner rides a single outbound endpoint.
  • Minus 10 points when the partner has been quiet for 14+ days.
  • All measured against the newest log day, then clamped to 0–100.
  • Partner attribution is the site-wide UNION rule (the subscription’s configured partners unioned with the parse attribution).
  • The concentration boxes (top-1/3/10 share, Gini) cover all seen partners; the scorecard only those with at least 100 Files. A top-N share = the distinct Files touching one of the N busiest partners, over every File with a partner (a File of two partners counts once).

Tips

  • The biggest byte mover is not the biggest file counter: the Entities › Partners view sorted on Volume or on Files gives both rankings, for any date range.
