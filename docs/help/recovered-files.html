Retries & resubmissions › Recovered files

What it shows

  • A recovered File carried at least one failed leg and still finished OK. The boxes: Recovered Files, Automatic, Manual, share of all Files, Failed transfers healed, and the Subscriptions, Protocols and Days involved.

Columns

  • Per subscription — Recovered = Automatic + Manual; Files = all its Files; Recovered %. The Unknown subscription is left out (it counts in the boxes and the other tables).
  • Per protocol — the same split, plus Failed transfers healed over all Failed transfers of that protocol = Healed %, how often a failure there is transient.
  • Per day — Files, Recovered, Automatic, Manual, Share %; only days with a recovery; the Date opens the day page.

Rules

  • Per subscription: rows tint by the subscription’s result colour.
  • Automatic = no operator (a platform retry, or a bookend-settled File); Manual = a leg carries the Resubmitted flag. They are the Transfer top view’s Recovered and the home page’s Cured.
  • Orange File rows elsewhere also cover a resubmitted File that never failed: orange, not Recovered.
  • OK = Processed or Waiting. A File counts on its start day.
  • Per protocol uses the failed leg’s protocol; failed legs on two protocols count under each, its Total once.
  • A narrowed date range hides a subscription or protocol with no recovered File in it.

Tips

  • A high Recovered % on a busy flow points at a flaky endpoint. Click a row for its 10 newest recovered Files.
