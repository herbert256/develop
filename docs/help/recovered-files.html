Recovered files

What it shows

A recovered File carried at least one failed transfer leg and still finished OK — a retry delivered it. Three tables: per subscription, per protocol of the healed failure, per day; two boxes at the top split Recovered into Retry and Resubmit.

Columns

  • Retry — the platform’s automatic retry delivered the File. Resubmit — an operator resubmitted it: a leg (the delivering one included) carries the Resubmitted flag. Retry + Resubmit = Recovered, the Top view’s Automatic / Manual. A blank cell is 0.
  • Files (per subscription) — all its Files, whatever their outcome. Recovered % — the share that needed a retry; a high share on a busy flow points at a flaky endpoint that succeeds on the second try.
  • Healed % (per protocol) — failed legs of recovered Files over all failed legs of that protocol: how often a failure there is transient.

Rules

  • Same rule and figures as the Top view’s Recovered columns, the home page’s Cured cell and the subscription detail pages: the File counts under Files/Ok, its failed legs under Transfers/Error.
  • OK per the site-wide outcome policy (Processed or Waiting; Failed and Expired are not OK). Everything is attributed to the File’s start day.
  • Per subscription: only subscriptions with at least one recovered File are listed.
  • Per protocol: the failed leg’s protocol — where the healed failure happened, not what delivered the File. Failed legs on two protocols count once under each, so the column can sum past the distinct Files.
  • Per day: only days with a recovery (the Top view shows every day); the Date cell opens that day’s page.
  • From/To re-aggregates the two upper tables from their per-day buckets; a subscription or protocol with zero recovered Files in range is hidden until the range widens.

Tips

  • Every row expands on click into its 10 most recent recovered Files, newest first, always the full period.
