Recovered files

What this shows

A recovered File carried at least one failed transfer leg and still finished OK — a retry delivered it. It is the same rule behind the Top view's Recovered columns (Automatic + Manual), the home page's Cured cell and the subscription detail pages, so the figures line up exactly: the File counts under Files/Ok while its failed legs count under Transfers/Error. OK follows the site-wide outcome policy (Processed or Waiting; Failed and Expired are not OK), and everything is attributed to the File's start day.

The three tables

How the File recovered is shown in every table and in two boxes at the top, splitting Recovered into Retry and Resubmit: Retry means the platform's own automatic retry delivered the File after the failed leg; Resubmit means an operator resubmitted it — one of the File's transfer legs carries the Resubmitted flag (the delivering leg included). It is the Top view's Automatic / Manual rule, so Retry + Resubmit always equals Recovered and the figures line up with the Top view's Recovered columns. A blank cell is 0.

Per subscription answers which flows heal themselves: the Files column counts all of the subscription's Files, Recovered % the share of them that needed a retry — a high share on a busy flow points at a flaky endpoint that succeeds on the second try. Only subscriptions with at least one recovered File are listed.

Per protocol looks at the failed leg's protocol — where the healed failure actually happened, not what finally delivered the File. Healed % compares the failed legs that belonged to recovered Files against all failed legs of that protocol: how often a failure there turns out to be transient. A File whose failed legs span two protocols counts once under each.

Per day lists only the days with at least one recovery (the Top view shows every day); the Date cell opens that day's page.

Every row expands on click into its 10 most recent recovered Files, newest first. The From/To range re-aggregates the two upper tables from their per-day buckets — a subscription or protocol with zero recovered files inside the selected range is hidden until the range widens again; the drill lists always show the full period.
