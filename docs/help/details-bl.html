BL Details

What this shows

This is the per-BL detail page — one page per BL: a tags entry of a configured subscription starting with BL, kept verbatim, or a BL number listed for it in input/BL.txt. It opens with a one-line KPI summary, then Activity per day, a breakdown of every dimension the tag's transfers touch (subscription, account, login, remote host, domain, application, logical, partner), the latest Files and the load tables. A file belongs to a BL through its subscription: every BL tag of the File’s subscription counts it.

The subscription / account / login / remote host tables are cross-referenced against the FlowManager configuration: every name configured for this tag is listed, whether it appears in the logs or not — green rows have logged data, red rows are configured but never seen, each linking to its own detail page.

Last error(s)

Directly above the Features table, a page carries a Last error(s) table whenever any subscription it moves files with has something wrong on it: one row per such subscription — its most recent error, newest first — with the date and time, the subscription and the file. Clicking anywhere on a row opens that file’s error page, with every transfer leg and the server log of the failure.

Direction, page colors and never-seen pages

Every configured BL has a page — seen in the logs or not. The page background color says the same at a glance: light brown = seen & in, light blue = seen & out, light gray = seen & both/unknown, light yellow = never seen & in, light orange = never seen & out, light purple = never seen & both/unknown.

How it's computed

The KPI line reports this tag's rank and share of traffic among all BL tags, its failure rate, total volume and cadence, plus processed-transfer performance where durations are recorded. Every count is in Files: one per logical transfer, split by its delivered (final-row) outcome, volume counted once per file.

A Partner, Application, Domain or Logical breakdown holding exactly one row is folded away: the name appears as a row in the Features table instead, keeping its link and colour.
