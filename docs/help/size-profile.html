Size profile

What it shows

What each flow’s files weigh: one row per subscription with 20+ Files — its overall median size, the median of its first half of files against its second half, and its share of stub files.

Rules

  • The halves are the flow’s own files split by count in chronological order (not calendar halves), so a burst-shaped flow is compared fairly.
  • Drift = second-half median ÷ first-half median; ∞ marks a flow whose first-half median was 0 bytes.
  • A drift of 3× or more in either direction is a regime change: a new format, a changed upstream export, or content replaced by placeholders — worth understanding, not by itself an error.
  • A stub is a file smaller than max(1 KB, 1% of the flow’s median); the per-flow limit is in the Stub limit column. A flow ships stubs when 10%+ of its files are below it.
  • Two-regime flows are often legitimate: a data file plus a 0-byte semaphore companion gives a steady ~50% stub share by design. The table shows the mix so an unexpected stub surge (real content replaced by empties) stands out — informational, not an alarm list.
