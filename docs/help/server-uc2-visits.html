UC2 pickup visits

What it shows

For every UC2 flow whose pickup account ever logged on: what the partner’s SSH visits did. A visit groups an account’s logons (a gap of more than 30 minutes starts a new one); each visit is classified by what its window shows in the transfer log.

Columns

  • Pickups — the account’s pickup logons (the detail page’s Pickup information Total pickups); higher than the visit columns, a visit holding several logons.
  • Collected — visits that took at least one staged file.
  • Collected + delivered — a two-way exchange: the partner dropped its own files (the UC4 twin flow) and took ours in one visit.
  • Delivered only — a UC4 delivery, not a pickup; excluded from every pickup figure on the site.
  • Same connection — distinct technical SSH connections (the transfer log’s Session ID) in which the account both delivered and collected a file. The only figure that raises the detail pages’ Connection shared with UC4 drop row and the Pickups report’s UC4 drop flag.
  • Files picked up — per subscription; the OK figure of its detail page.

Rules

  • Classification is per visit, not per logon: every connection of a visit inherits its class. A visit that collected counts as a pickup even when it also delivered.
  • Same connection counts sessions, not visits: an SFTP client typically opens a fresh connection per operation, so a two-way visit whose uploads and downloads travelled separately shows 0.
  • The pickup, visit and session figures are the account’s and repeat on each of its UC2 subscriptions — except on an account with several FE logins (production), where they are the flow’s own login’s.
  • Each subscription links to its detail page, whose Pickup information table (first/last pickup, pattern, delivering logons) carries that flow’s own figures.
  • A partner collecting over CFT/PESIT logs no SSH visit: absent here while its files are collected fine (Files picked up still shows on its detail page). A flow whose account never logged on is not listed.
  • The same per-visit classification feeds UC2 status and the detail pages (the uc2-pickups sidecar), so the three never disagree.
  • Logons = the server log’s successful-SSH-logon lines; deliveries = the account’s inbound SSH transfer records; collections = each staged File’s final successful outbound (download) record. A session counts once when it carries at least one delivery and one successful collection of the account’s files.
