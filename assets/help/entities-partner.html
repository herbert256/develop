Partners

What it shows

  • One row per external partner organisation, in the shared Entities layout.

Rules

  • A partner is the last part of the logical name domain_application_partner; input/logical_partners.txt rewrites a token first, and tokens sharing endpoints, whitelist IPs or whitelisted host addresses merge into one organisation.
  • A File counts for the partner its subscription names (else its host’s, else its account’s) and for every configured partner of its subscription; the Total row, narrowed dates included, counts each File once.
  • A merged name carries 🔗, linking its Partner group page.
