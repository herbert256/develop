What is new

What it shows

  • From the git history of the report generators: the 25 most recently added reports (New reports) and the 25 most recently changed ones (Changed reports), newest first.

Columns

  • Date — the commit day. Report — a link to the page. Group — its group of the Reports menu. Description (New reports) — the report’s one-line description. Change (Changed reports) — the subject line of its latest commit.

Rules

  • A change counts only when its commit touched at most eight generators; a sweep across more is about the site, not a report.
  • A report in New reports is not repeated in Changed reports.
  • Unpublished intermediates (the components of merged reports) never appear.

Tips

  • Reached from the Site map’s Tools card.
