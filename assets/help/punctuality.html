Punctuality

What this shows

Does the daily file arrive on time? For every subscription active on 8+ days, the day's first File defines that day's arrival time. The median over the window is the flow's typical slot, and the spread (one standard deviation) classifies its regularity:

  • Clockwork — ±15 minutes or tighter. Cron-driven flows land here, many at ±0 min: the observed side of the schedules the Cronjobs analysis extracts from the configuration.
  • Regular — within an hour.
  • Loose — within three hours.
  • Irregular — wider: event-driven flows, where "late" and "missed" have no meaning (those columns show "-").

For Clockwork and Regular flows it then flags Late arrivals (over an hour past the typical slot) and Missed days: a weekday the flow served on 75%+ of its calendar occurrences that passed without any File. Click a row with late/missed counts for the exact dates.

How it's computed

Per (subscription, day) the earliest File time is kept; the median of those minutes is the typical slot. The missed-day model builds each flow's weekday profile (which weekdays it usually serves) and walks the whole calendar window — so a Tuesday flow that skips a Tuesday is flagged, while never running on weekends is not. The page always shows the full period (the model needs the whole window), so there is no From/To filter.

How it relates to the other timing reports

Stale Accounts measures idle days per account against its own cadence — day granularity, account level. This page is time-of-day granularity at subscription level: the file arrived, but an hour late; the Tuesday slot passed empty. Load by Hour shows when the platform is busy overall; this shows whether each individual rhythm holds.
