Server top view

What it shows

  • The whole server log per calendar day (not the transfer log): every figure counts log lines, not Files. Days without a line get a zero row.
  • Below it, Levels per component: the level split per component over the selected range.
  • The summary line under the tables: days, records, errors (with their share), warnings and the noisiest component.

Columns

  • Date; Records with its Load bar.
  • Info / Warnings (amber) / Errors (red) / Error % = Errors ÷ Records.
  • TM (transaction manager) / PESITD / SSHD — records per component.
  • First / Last — the day’s first and last record time.
  • Levels per component — Component, Info, Warnings, Errors, Records.

Rules

  • Levels other than Info / Warning / Error (none expected) count in Records only.
  • Every column is additive and re-totals under the date filter; the page opens at the full period with the total row on top.

Tips

  • Click a day or a component for its 10 most recent Warning/Error lines; a day with none is not clickable.
  • To explain a spike, open Errors (Log reasons, Top messages) and narrow it to that day.
