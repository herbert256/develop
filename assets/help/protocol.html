Protocol, Direction & Mode

What it shows

  • Transfer legs broken down by the per-leg attributes of the transfer log, one tab each. A summary line gives the OK transfers and their volume — the tables’ own totals.

Tabs

  • Protocol × direction — Protocol (pesit, ssh, ftp, routing …), Direction, Transfers, Volume, % of transfers.
  • Direction × action by — Direction, Action By (who initiated the leg: User or Server), Transfers.
  • Mode — the log’s Mode column (BINARY / ASCII), Transfers.

Rules

  • Counts transfers (legs), not Files: a File’s Inbound leg (say ssh) and Outbound leg (say pesit) count once each.
  • Transfers = the OK legs only; Volume = the bytes of those OK legs; the share is over the OK legs. A row with no OK leg is left out, on every tab.
  • UNKNOWN = nothing logged for that attribute.
  • A protocol’s or direction’s own total is the sum of its rows: search or sort the table.
