Partner groups & the Direction column

What a partner group is

Most partner names on the Entities → Partners pages are a single organisation — ROTAFORM, VECOZO. Some are a group: a name like GBA_TT_XXLLNC that joins several partner codes with underscores. FlowManager configures those codes as separate accounts, but the grouping logic recognises them as one real partner organisation and counts them together. A group row carries a small 🔗 link icon after its name; clicking it opens a page that lists the group's member codes and the exact evidence used to merge them.

How partners are grouped

Each account name is first split into domain · application · partner parts, giving every account a partner token. Those tokens are then merged into one group whenever any of three things holds, applied repeatedly until nothing more combines:

  • Two inbound partners share a whitelist IP — two accounts the partner connects into SecureTransport with both allow the same source address, so they are the same partner reaching us two ways.
  • An outbound host is whitelisted for an inbound partner — an account SecureTransport dials out to lands on an IP that an inbound account's whitelist allows: the same partner, both directions.
  • An outbound host's domain matches a partner name — the endpoint SecureTransport delivers to (e.g. …rabobank.nl) carries a second-level domain equal to a known partner code.

The group's name is its member codes joined with underscores, sorted alphabetically. The per-group page groups the raw evidence by the pair of partner codes it links, showing one representative fact per pair with a count of the rest.

The Direction column

The Partners list carries a Direction column reading connection / movement — for example both/both or in/out. Connection is the partner's configured side: in (the partner connects into SecureTransport), out (SecureTransport connects out to the partner) or both. Movement is which way the files actually travel across the partner's subscriptions (in, out, or both). The two diverge on pull flows — a partner SecureTransport connects out to in order to collect files that move in reads out/in.
