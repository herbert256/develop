Account sharing

What this shows

Which accounts serve more than one subscription, and in what shape. An account is a partner relation (with its FE login riding along 1-to-1), and one relation often carries several flows — but only in a few recurring patterns:

Mailbox pair — exactly UC2 + UC4: the partner’s inbound mailbox, one connection used to drop (UC4) and pick up (UC2). This is the only inbound sharing that occurs — never two UC2s or two UC4s — and it is why UC2/UC4 detail pages carry the shared-connection notes and why such pairs are Twins. Outbound pair — exactly UC1 + UC3: the outbound mirror, we push to and poll the same partner. Outbound fan-out — several UC1/UC3 flows over one relation, up to dozens. Both directions — inbound and outbound use cases on one account: real partners never do this; the synthetic ST end-to-end monitor account is built this way on purpose.

Reading the table

Only the shared accounts are listed (the single-subscription majority is counted in the boxes above). Per account: its kind, how many subscriptions of each use case, and the full subscription list (three or more fold behind an ellipsis — click to expand). The account links to its detail page. Rows are grouped by kind, largest first.

How it's computed

Configuration only: the account → subscription pairs from the FlowManager export (the xref cache), classified by the subscription names’ UC<n> prefix (UC1/UC3 = we connect out, UC2/UC4 = the partner connects in). No log data is involved, so the page is identical however much traffic ran.
