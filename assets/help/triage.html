Triage

What it shows

One ranked worklist instead of six symptom pages: every subscription that needs eyes now. One row per subscription, its symptoms in one line and an Evidence pointer onward.

Columns

  • Symptoms — red (last File failed, or server-log evidence after the last OK), expiry risk (staged Waiting Files 9+ days old; the retention sweep deletes at ~11) or just went quiet (last File 8–13 days before the window end).
  • Priority red > expiry risk > just went quiet; any second condition is mentioned in the symptoms.
  • Red-since — the server-log evidence stamp where one exists, else the first failure of the current failing run.
  • Days in state — counted against the last day in the data, never the wall clock.
  • Volume in window — the flow’s volume over the loaded window: historical throughput, not an undelivered backlog.
  • At expiry risk — the staged, uncollected bytes the sweep is about to delete (expiry-risk rows only).
  • Evidence — where to read on.

Rules

  • Rank score = lifetime Files / (days in state + 1): state recency times historical weight. Yesterday’s flip on a busy flow outranks a month-old red, which outranks a one-file wonder.
  • A triage desk, not a diagnosis: From green to red, Went quiet, Waiting, Expired, Only red and the Boxes pages remain the deep-dives, linked at the bottom.
