Twins

What this shows

Every Twin relation the detail pages know, collated on one page — each pair listed once (the relation is symmetric; the detail pages carry it on both sides).

A subscription twin is the same flow configured the opposite way: the UC2+UC4 mailbox pair (the partner drops and picks up over one connection) or the UC1+UC3 outbound pair (we push to and poll the same partner). A pair is detected by the union of three rules: by name (same name body, opposite side), by account (the two flows share their account — the mailbox pair, or an account whose only two subscriptions are one UC1 and one UC3), or via spelling-twin accounts (each side’s account is the other spelled with the other separator). The “Detected by” column says which; a pair proven only by the shared account is a naming slip — the flows belong together but their names disagree, which is the rename backlog.

An account twin is one partner relation configured twice, under both separator spellings (- vs _) — the same pairs the Separator collision analysis and Config hygiene track; renaming one side merges the pair.

The Login column

The FE… service login the pair connects with, from the FlowManager configuration. A mailbox pair usually shares ONE login — that is the twin relation seen from the credential side — and the single value links to the login’s detail page. A pair whose sides carry different logins lists both, and an out-only pair (UC1+UC3) shows -: outbound flows hold the remote credential in the communication profile, not in a login.

Every column sorts — the page opens with the naming slips first, and a click on any header re-orders; the detection and use-case boxes above still narrow the rows.

How it's computed

A formatter over the pair maps the detail-page build persists, so this page and the detail pages’ Twin rows can never disagree. Configuration only — no log data is involved.
