Partner scorecard

What it shows

  • A 0–100 health score per partner with at least 100 Files, worst first, every ingredient in its own column.
  • Boxes: partners seen and scored, the Top-1 / Top-3 / Top-10 share of Files and the Gini coefficient — how heavily the estate leans on a few partners.

Columns

  • Score, Files, Error % (Failed or Expired), Volume, Last seen.
  • Trend (14d) — the last 14 days’ Error % against the 14 before: ▲ worse, ▼ better (over 1 point), ― steady or too little data.
  • Avg pickup wait — UC2 Files only (UC2-named, or a flow derived as UC2). Weakest security — ssh-rsa and/or TLSv1.2, with its share of the legs.
  • Hosts — distinct endpoints we dial. Direction — in, out or two-way, by connection side.

Rules

  • Score = 100 − Error %, minus 0.5 per point the trend worsened (max 15), 5 when the pickup wait averages over 24 h, up to 5 for the weak-security share, 3 for a single endpoint, 10 when quiet 14+ days; clamped 0–100.
  • Row tint: score under 40 red, under 70 orange, else green.
  • A File counts for its own partner and every configured partner of its subscription. The share boxes cover all seen partners, each File once.
