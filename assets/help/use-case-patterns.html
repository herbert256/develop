Use Case patterns

What this shows

Every account grouped by its Use Case pattern: the mix of its configured subscriptions, counted per UC prefix. An account whose subscriptions are UC2_SI-VPS-VDN_SI-VPS-VDN and UC4_SI-VPS-VDN_SI-VPS-VDN has one UC2 and one UC4 subscription, so its pattern is UC2 (1) UC4 (1). Accounts sharing the exact same mix land on the same row, so the table answers "which subscription set-ups exist, and how common is each?" — the single-subscription patterns (UC1 (1), UC4 (1)) dominate, while the long tail shows the poll-heavy accounts with many UC3 subscriptions.

The Accounts column loads collapsed to a single count line ("36 accounts" — the same mechanism as the coverage pages' member cells). Click the count to expand the alphabetical list, one account per line, each linked to its own detail page; click again to collapse. Sorting the column orders by the count.

How it's computed

The account → subscription pairs come from the FlowManager configuration export (the data/flow-manager/xref/ pair cache built from subscriptions.json) — configured subscriptions, whether they ever appear in the logs or not. Each subscription contributes the leading UC<n> token of its name; a subscription without a UC prefix counts under a (none) token, and an account with no subscriptions at all has no pattern and is not listed. The pattern string orders the UC tokens numerically, each with its subscription count in parentheses. Rows are ordered by how many accounts share the pattern, most first; the total row counts the distinct patterns and the accounts listed.
