AV Scan

What it shows

  • The anti-virus (ICAP) verdict of every File, taken from its first Inbound leg: the scan runs when a file enters the system.

Tabs

  • Breakdown — Outcome (Allowed, Not performed, Blocked, Error), Files, Share.
  • Per day — Date, Allowed, Blocked, Not performed, Other, Total.
  • Per protocol — the same columns per protocol of the first Inbound leg: pesit = from CFT, ssh/ftp = a partner delivering in.
  • Blocked — the 200 newest blocked Files: Date, Time, Account, Login, Subscription, Protocol, File.
  • Not performed — Reason, Files, Share; a row drills to its 10 newest Files.
  • Not first inbound — Direction, Protocol, Outcome, Transfers: verdicts found on any other leg.

Rules

  • One verdict per File; a File without an Inbound leg has none. Parsed from the free-text ICAP Details column; Other / Error = Error, Unknown or unrecognised text.
  • Not performed reasons: Before scanning was active (ahead of the first Allowed verdict, its time in the label), Failed or empty arrival, Encrypted (.pgp / .enc), Oversized (≥ 1 MB), Other — should stay near 0; growth means a new skip reason.
  • Not first inbound: inbound retries and the UC2 staging re-entry (inbound routing) are expected; an outbound row would break the scan-on-entry model. The outbound “Scanning was not performed” boilerplate is left out.
