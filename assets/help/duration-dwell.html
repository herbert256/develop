Distribution & Store-and-forward

What it shows

  • Two clocks on the same Files, side by side: the whole trip (Duration distribution) and the wait inside SecureTransport between the inbound leg ending and the outbound leg starting (Dwell).
  • The summary line gives the measurable Files and their median, p95 and max dwell.

Columns

  • Duration distribution — Duration bucket / Files / Share, seven bands from <= 100 ms to > 30 min; a Delivered Files / All Files switch (All adds the failed Files, each as long as it ran before giving up).
  • Dwell-time distribution — Dwell / Files / Share, bands from < 1 s to over 5 min.
  • Dwell by subscription — Subscription / Files / Avg dwell / Max dwell, slowest first; a row drills to its 10 newest Files.
  • Gap per day — Date / Files / Min / Median / Max / P25 / P50 / P75 / P95 / P99 of the in→out gap, under the inbound date.

Rules

  • Duration = the File’s wall-clock span, first record start to last record end.
  • Dwell = the earliest outbound start minus the latest inbound completion (start + duration). A File forwarded before its inbound leg finished (overlapping legs) is excluded.
  • Gap per day counts only delivered Files with exactly one Inbound and one Outbound record, both Processed; its figures are not additive, so a narrowed range blanks the total row.
  • Percentiles use the nearest-rank method; Median = P50.

Tips

  • A growing dwell, invisible in both leg durations, is an early warning of queue pressure or a backed-up outbound target.
