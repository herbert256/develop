First seen

What it shows

  • For six entity types — Logical, Partners, Subscriptions, Accounts, Logins, Hosts — the day each configured name first appeared in the transfer log: one row per log day, newest first.
  • Above the days: Total, Seen, Not seen (opening the matching Entities view) and Seen, no date.
  • A day cell opens a page of its names — each linking its detail page, with its First transfer timestamp, tinted by its result colour.

Rules

  • Per column: Seen + Not seen = Total; the day rows + Seen, no date = Seen.
  • Transfers no subscription claimed (subscription Unknown) are no subscription and are not counted; a Total or Seen cell equals the Entities view it opens.
  • Seen, no date = seen without a dated transfer of its own, credited through a sibling (a shared endpoint’s co-tenant, one organisation of a two-partner account).
  • Only transfers count: a name the server log mentions but that never transferred is Not seen.
  • Dates: accounts and partners per File; subscriptions, logins and hosts per log record. Names match exactly, case aside; a subscription also matches the logged names it prefixes.

Tips

  • The first log day is naturally the largest row: everything already running is first seen there.
